## R-20261120-1: seed (2026-11-20)

Seeded from the planning session that started this plugin and from general knowledge; entries marked *unverified* need a primary-source check on the first refresh.

### Car seats
- Car-seat rules are state law and depend on the child's age (and often height or weight) **at the time of travel**, not at booking. Example: New York requires a child restraint until the 8th birthday (NY VTL 1229-c; checked in session 2026-11-20 via NY DMV/GTSC pages). Other states differ (several to age 8, some to 9, some by height 4'9"). *Recheck per state at use.*
- Rideshare: Uber and Lyft drivers are not required to supply seats; Uber Car Seat exists only in some cities (NYC, LA). *Superseded by R-20261120-2.*
- NYC taxis are exempt from the car-seat law; rideshare treatment varies. *Verified in R-20261120-2.*

### TSA
- Liquids: 3.4 oz (100 ml) containers in one quart bag per traveller; medically needed liquids, formula and breast milk allowed in larger amounts, declared at screening. Source: tsa.gov "What Can I Bring?". *Recheck yearly.*
- Children under 18 travelling with an adult need no ID on domestic flights. Adults need a REAL ID-compliant ID since 2025-05-07. Source: tsa.gov/real-id.
- Medicines: allowed in pills or solid form in unlimited amounts if screened; original labelled bottles are advised and some states require them. Source: tsa.gov medications page.

### Cannabis and controlled medicines
- Cannabis stays illegal under federal law, and TSA refers finds to local police; legal state to legal state is still federal ground at the airport. Hemp-derived and FDA-approved (Epidiolex) products are the exception. Source: tsa.gov "Medical marijuana". *Recheck: rescheduling news.*
- Schedule II stimulants (common ADHD medicines) travel fine domestically in original bottles; abroad, check the destination country's rules (Japan, UAE are strict). *Japan and UAE verified in R-20261120-2 / R-20261120-3.*

### Rideshare against rental, heuristics
- Count rides a day × days × typical fare (check the app for the real pair) plus the airport legs at surge times; add tips (about 15–20%).
- Rental: days × base rate + taxes and airport fees (often 20–35% on top) + parking at the stay and destinations + car-seat rental ($10–15 a day each, capped) or bring your own (airlines check car seats free).
- Rental usually wins with 3+ rides a day for a family of 4+ outside a dense city; rideshare wins in cities where parking is hard. Show the arithmetic every time.

### Weather
- Daily forecast detail is reliable to about 7 days out; NWS point forecasts go 7 days (weather.gov). Past that, give the monthly normals as typical and say so.

## R-20261120-2: first refresh (2026-11-20)

- Uber Car Seat (NYC, LA): seat is now a Nuna RAVA convertible, rear- or forward-facing, about 5-65 lb; UberX fare plus a surcharge (about $10 in NYC); parent installs and is responsible for fit. Old "forward-facing only, age 2+" guidance is outdated; Uber pages still disagree on minimums, so check uber.com/info/nyc-car-seat at use. Source: uber.com/info/nyc-car-seat, uber.com/us/en/blog/car-seat-faq. Magnitude: medium (rear-facing infants now possible).
- NYC taxis and liveries: child under 8 may ride without a restraint (state VTL exemption; NYC 311 KA-03372: not required, driver must let you install your own). Seat belts still apply from age 8. Whether Uber/Lyft share the exemption is unclear; bill S890 (2023) to remove the exemption had not passed. Source: portal.311.nyc.gov KA-03372, nyc.gov TLC passenger FAQ, nysenate.gov S890. Magnitude: small (confirms seed).
- Japan: amphetamine (Adderall, Dexedrine) cannot be imported even with a prescription (Stimulants Control Act); lisdexamfetamine (Vyvanse) and some others need advance permission from the Narcotics Control Department. Source: mhlw.go.jp personal-use medicines page, ncd.mhlw.go.jp application guidance. Magnitude: medium (plan must flag "do not carry" for amphetamines to Japan, not just "check").

## R-20261120-3: UAE controlled medicines (2026-11-20)

- UAE: controlled and semi-controlled medicines need approval. Visitors may apply for electronic approval before travel (optional for visitors) or, without it, must declare the medicine on arrival with the prescription and a medical report. Transit passengers who stay in the port need no approval. The official list (200+ medicines) marks which need a permit and which are prohibited; check the named medicine there. The personal-import permit service reportedly moved from MOHAP to the Emirates Drug Establishment on 2025-12-29; check which agency hosts it at use. Source: mohap.gov.ae "Issue of Permit to Import Medicines for Personal Use", u.ae drugs-and-controlled-medicines page, uae-embassy.org medication page, Khaleej Times on the agency move. Magnitude: small (confirms "strict"; adds the declare-on-arrival route). Not verified: per-medicine status of amphetamine salts on the list (2026-11-20 retry: the MOHAP list PDF reset the connection and u.ae returned 404; secondary sources only say ADHD stimulants are controlled, some amphetamine products reportedly prohibited, none name which). Until checked, the plan says: look the generic name up on the list and get e-approval before flying; do not rely on declaring at arrival.

## R-20261120-4: documents, fees and scope (2026-11-20)

- REAL ID: enforced since 2025-05-07. Since 2026-02-01 an adult (18+) with no REAL ID or other accepted ID (passport) pays a $45 TSA ConfirmID fee (pay.gov or at the airport, covers 10 days, non-refundable, verification can still fail). Children under 18 travelling domestically with an adult need no ID and pay no fee. Source: tsa.gov/real-id and ConfirmID announcement as reported by ABC7 Chicago and NBC Chicago (Jan 2026), Washington Times (2025-12-01). Magnitude: medium (new per-adult cost line).
- Passports: every traveller abroad needs one, children included. A US passport issued under 16 is valid 5 years; applications under 16 need both parents (or consent form DS-3053) in person on DS-11. Many countries want 6 months of validity left; check the destination page on travel.state.gov. Source: travel.state.gov minors page. Magnitude: small.
- Scope ruling: points, miles and award booking stay out (the skill is not for booking or price shopping). Fees that change the plan are in: ConfirmID, bag fees and car-seat or rental extras inside the rideshare against rental arithmetic (Rule 3). Major US airlines check car seats and strollers free; confirm on the airline's page at use.
